How to redact a driver’s license or passport photo

On an ID, the number you can read is only half the problem: the barcode on the back of most US licenses and the two lines of characters at the bottom of a passport (the machine-readable zone) encode your name, number and date of birth again. Cover the ID number, the barcode or machine-readable zone, and your signature; keep only what the recipient needs, usually your name and photo. PDF Redact finds your name, address and date of birth in the photo; draw boxes over the rest.

  1. In PDF Redact, tap Open PDF or Photo and pick the photo from your Photo Library.
  2. The app reads the text on the card and boxes the name, address and date of birth it finds. Tap any of them to turn it off if the recipient needs it.
  3. Use the Rectangle tool to cover the license or passport number, the barcode, the passport’s machine-readable zone and your signature. ID numbers don’t follow one format, so cover them by hand.
  4. Export. The result is a PDF, which email and most upload forms accept.

What each part gives away

  • ID number: the key to your record; the most important thing to hide.
  • Barcode (back of a US license): name, address, date of birth and license number in machine-readable form.
  • Machine-readable zone (passport): name, passport number, nationality, date of birth and expiry.
  • Signature: rarely needed by the recipient.

Identity checks required by law (banks, employers verifying work eligibility, some landlords’ screening services) often need the full, unredacted document through their own secure upload. Don’t email a full ID to someone you haven’t verified.

Questions

Can I just cover things with Markup in Photos?
Drawing an opaque shape on a photo and saving it does hide those pixels. A PDF is different: shapes drawn on a PDF usually leave the text underneath. Either way, remember the barcode and machine-readable zone.
Does it work on a scan of both sides?
Yes. Open the PDF and redact each page.
