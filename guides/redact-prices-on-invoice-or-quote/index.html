How to hide prices on an invoice or quote

To share a quote or invoice without the prices, remove every amount from the file, not just the ones you can see on the first page. In PDF Redact, open the detection settings, choose your own categories and turn on Amounts: every dollar amount is found and boxed. Add line items or totals by hand if needed, then export. The amounts are deleted from the PDF, so they can’t be copied or searched.

  1. Open the quote or invoice in PDF Redact.
  2. In the detection settings, choose your own categories and turn on Amounts. Leave on anything else you want hidden, such as account numbers.
  3. Check the totals, taxes and any amounts written in words. Double-tap a line to redact it, or draw a Rectangle over a whole price column.
  4. To hide a vendor or customer name everywhere, use Find & Redact and redact every match.
  5. Export and send the verified file.

Why highlighting isn’t enough

Drawing a black shape with a markup or annotation tool is not redaction. The shape sits on top of the page and the text underneath usually stays in the file, where anyone can select, copy or search it. Real redaction deletes the text and any hidden copies of it. Prices are the classic case: a recipient can select the black bar, copy, and paste the total into a note.

Questions

Will the totals still add up visually?
No. Totals are amounts too and get redacted. If the recipient needs the total, tap its redaction to turn it off.
Can I use white boxes so it looks clean?
Yes. Choose white as the box style; the text underneath is still removed.
