How to redact medical records

PDF Redact has two medical presets. Medical / HIPAA removes names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, SSNs, dates of birth, account numbers and record or member numbers while keeping the clinical content. HIPAA Safe Harbor goes further and targets all 18 Safe Harbor identifiers, adding every date and record, member and account number. Everything is processed on the device; nothing is uploaded.

Steps

  1. Open the record in PDF Redact.
  2. Open the detection settings and pick the Medical / HIPAA or HIPAA Safe Harbor preset.
  3. Review every page. Add anything the detector can’t judge: photos of faces, a rare diagnosis that identifies someone, a small town name in free text.
  4. Export and keep the Verified Clean file.

A note on de-identification

HIPAA’s Safe Harbor method (45 CFR 164.514(b)) requires removing 18 kinds of identifiers and having no actual knowledge that the rest could identify the person. Software can find identifiers, but you (or your privacy officer) remain responsible for the final judgment. Always review before sharing.

Questions

Are the documents sent anywhere?
No. Detection and redaction run entirely on your device.
Does it work on scanned records?
Yes, with on-device text recognition. Check scans carefully; poor scans can hide text from any tool.
