How to redact a pay stub

A pay stub proves income through the employer name, pay period, gross and net pay and year-to-date totals; keep those. Hide your Social Security number, employee ID and the bank account your pay goes to. PDF Redact finds the SSN, account numbers, names and addresses automatically; turn off anything the recipient needs to see, then export.

  1. Download the pay stub as a PDF from your payroll portal, or take a clear photo of a paper stub.
  2. Open it in PDF Redact and review the automatic redactions.
  3. Tap to add your employee ID or any other number you want hidden. Tap your name’s redaction to turn it off if the recipient needs to match it to you.
  4. Export the verified file.

Keep visible

  • Employer name
  • Pay period and pay date
  • Gross pay, net pay and year-to-date totals
  • Your name (so the recipient can match it to you)

Usually hide

  • Social Security number (often already shown as the last four)
  • Employee or file number
  • Direct-deposit bank account and routing numbers
  • Home address, if not required

Questions

Will a landlord accept a redacted pay stub?
Usually, if income, dates and your name are readable. Ask first if you’re unsure.
