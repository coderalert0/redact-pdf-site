How to redact a PDF without uploading it

Web-based PDF tools work by uploading your file to the provider’s servers. For a document full of account numbers or medical details, that defeats the purpose. To keep the file on your device, use an app that redacts locally: PDF Redact does detection, redaction and its privacy audit on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, with no account and no upload. It works in airplane mode.

What to look for in an offline redaction tool

  • On-device processing: the document never leaves your device, not even for “AI” detection.
  • True removal: the text under each box is deleted, not covered.
  • Metadata and hidden-content cleanup: author fields, comments and hidden text are stripped.
  • A way to check the result: an audit or at least a search test.

Steps

  1. Turn on airplane mode if you want proof that nothing leaves the device.
  2. Open the PDF in PDF Redact; detection runs locally.
  3. Review, export, and share the verified file when you’re ready.
