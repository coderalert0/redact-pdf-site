How to redact a PDF on iPad

On iPad, open the PDF in PDF Redact, review the redactions it finds automatically, add your own with a tap or a rectangle, and export. The export removes the underlying text and passes a privacy audit before you share it. For a stack of documents, use Batch Redact.

  1. Open PDF Redact and tap Open PDF or Photo, or share a PDF to it from Files or Mail.
  2. Review the automatic redactions. Tap any of them to turn it off.
  3. Tap a word, double-tap a line, or draw a rectangle to add your own. Use Find & Redact to catch every occurrence of a name or number.
  4. Choose the box style if you need one: black, white, blur or pixelate.
  5. Export. The privacy audit runs automatically and reports Verified Clean when the file is safe to share.

Redacting many documents

From the home screen, choose Batch Redact and select several PDFs or photos. The app runs detection on each one; tap any document to preview its redactions, then export them together. Batch redaction needs a subscription. See batch redaction.

Questions

Which iPads are supported?
Any iPad that runs iPadOS 17 or later.
Is the redaction permanent?
Yes. The exported pages are rebuilt from what is visible, so redacted text is removed, not hidden behind a box.
Can I use it offline?
Yes. Everything runs on the iPad; no connection is needed to redact.
