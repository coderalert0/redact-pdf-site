How to redact a financial disclosure for family court

Financial disclosures in divorce and custody cases are full of account numbers, SSNs and children’s details. When a copy is filed with the court and becomes part of the public record, most courts allow only the last four digits of SSNs and account numbers, the year of birth and a minor’s initials. PDF Redact’s court-filing format applies the number and date rules automatically.

Filed versus exchanged

Redaction rules usually apply to what you file with the court. Copies you serve on the other party are often required to be complete. Check your state’s rules or your court’s self-help center; many states follow the same last-four-digits rule as the federal courts (for example California Rules of Court, rule 1.201).

Steps

  1. Open the affidavit or disclosure in PDF Redact. It recognises legal documents and applies the Legal / Court preset.
  2. Open the detection settings and turn on Court filing format. SSNs, taxpayer-ID and account numbers keep their last four digits; dates of birth keep the year.
  3. Children’s names are fully covered; uncover their initials by hand if your court wants them.
  4. Check attachments such as bank statements and pay stubs inside the same PDF; the preset covers them too.
  5. Export. Unredacted text stays searchable, which many e-filing systems require.
