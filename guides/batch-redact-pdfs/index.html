How to redact many PDFs at once

In PDF Redact, choose Batch Redact, select the PDFs or photos, and the app runs automatic detection on each one using the categories you choose. Tap any document to preview its redactions, then export them all. Batch redaction is part of the subscription.

  1. On the home screen, tap Batch Redact and select several PDFs or photos.
  2. Choose which kinds of information to detect for the whole batch.
  3. Tap a document to preview and adjust its redactions.
  4. Export the batch and share or save the files.

Batch work is quickest on a Mac, and the Mac app comes with the same purchase.

PDF RedactWorks on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Your first export is free.

No. The free export covers one document; batch needs a subscription.
