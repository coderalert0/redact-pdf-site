How to redact a Social Security number from a PDF

Open the PDF in PDF Redact: Social Security numbers are detected automatically in every preset, whether written 123-45-6789, 123 45 6789 or 123456789. Export, and the digits are removed from the file. If a court or agency wants the last four digits, turn on the court-filing format first.

  1. Open the document in PDF Redact on iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  2. Check that every SSN is boxed, including in headers, footers and form fields. Use Find & Redact with the last four digits to catch any copy written differently.
  3. Need only the last four? Open the detection settings (Filters, or Auto-redact in newer versions) and turn on Court filing format; it leaves the last four digits of SSNs, taxpayer-ID and account numbers and the year of birth visible.
  4. Export. The audit confirms no redacted digits remain anywhere in the file.

Where SSNs hide

  • Form fields (typed values on W-4s, I-9s, applications).
  • Scanned pages and photos, which need text recognition to find.
  • Repeated headers and footers on every page.
  • Document metadata and comments, which a box drawn on the page doesn’t touch.

Questions

Does it catch SSNs in scanned documents?
Yes. Scans and photos are read with on-device text recognition before detection runs.
What about ITINs and EINs?
Taxpayer identification numbers are covered too; the Tax Documents preset adds EINs.
