How to redact a utility bill for proof of address

A proof-of-address check needs your name, the service address, the provider and a recent date, and nothing else. Hide the account number, any reference or customer numbers, and the payment barcode. In PDF Redact, pick the Financial Statement preset in the detection settings: it keeps your name and address visible and removes account and reference numbers. Then export the verified file.

What to keep and what to hide

KeepHide
Your nameAccount and customer numbers
Service addressReference and meter numbers
Provider name and bill dateThe payment slip barcode or QR code
Usage history and amounts, if the recipient doesn’t need them

Steps

  1. Download the bill as a PDF from your provider’s website, or photograph the paper bill flat and in good light.
  2. Open it in PDF Redact. Open the detection settings and choose Financial Statement. Unlike the default preset, it leaves your name and address visible.
  3. Check the boxes. Draw a rectangle over the payment slip’s barcode or QR code, which can encode your account number.
  4. Export and send the file that reports Verified Clean.
PDF RedactWorks on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Your first export is free.

Questions

Will a bank or agency accept a redacted utility bill?
Usually, if your name, address, provider and date are readable. Some require an unredacted copy; ask first.
Why not use the default preset?
The default Standard PII preset redacts names and addresses, which are exactly what a proof of address has to show.
