PDF redaction tools compared: Acrobat, Preview, online tools and PDF Redact

For occasional, manual redaction on a Mac, Preview’s built-in Redact tool is free and removes what you mark. Adobe Acrobat Pro is the full-featured standard, with pattern search and document sanitizing, at about $20 a month. Free online tools are convenient but generally upload your file. PDF Redact sits in between: it finds personal information automatically on iPhone, iPad and Mac, works offline, and checks every export; the first export is free.

Which to use

  • A few boxes on a Mac, once: Preview (Tools → Redact). Not Markup rectangles.
  • You already pay for Acrobat Pro, or need full PDF editing: Acrobat’s Redact and Sanitize tools.
  • A non-sensitive file and no app to install: an online tool, after reading its privacy policy.
  • Sensitive documents on your phone, finding everything automatically, or court and HIPAA formats: PDF Redact.

Whatever you use, check the result

Questions

Is iPhone Markup a redaction tool?
No. Markup draws on top of a PDF; the text underneath usually stays in the file.
Does PDF Redact edit PDFs otherwise?
No. It redacts: it removes text, images and hidden data, then rebuilds a clean, searchable PDF.
