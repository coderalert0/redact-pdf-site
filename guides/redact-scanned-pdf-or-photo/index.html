How to redact a scanned PDF or a photo of a document

A scan or photo has no text layer, so most tools can’t search it. PDF Redact runs on-device text recognition first, then finds personal information in the recognised text and boxes it on the image. Open the photo from your Photo Library or the scan from Files, review, and export.

  1. In PDF Redact, tap Open PDF or Photo and choose Photo Library or Files.
  2. Wait for text recognition and detection to finish, then review the boxes.
  3. If the app says it couldn’t read the photo, retake it flat, in good light, filling the frame, and try again.
  4. Add anything it missed with the Rectangle tool, then export.

Tips for better results

  • Photograph the page flat and square-on; avoid shadows and glare.
  • Use a PDF from the source (bank, employer, court) instead of a photo whenever you can.
  • Small print on low-resolution scans is the most common thing any tool misses. Check it by eye.
